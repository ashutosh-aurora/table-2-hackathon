# Team context — Internal request triage (worked example)

## The ambition (one sentence)

An assistant that triages every inbound internal request, checks it is complete, asks the few
follow-up questions needed, and routes it to the right team.

## Who are the users

Two groups. **Requesters:** anyone across Aurora who sends an internal request (for data, access, a
fix, a report) and is not sure who owns it or what to include. **Receiving teams:** the people who
get those requests and currently spend time chasing missing detail or bouncing misrouted ones on.

## What does success look like

Requests arrive at the right team, first time, with enough detail to act on. Requesters stop guessing
who to ask. Receiving teams stop chasing basics and stop forwarding things that were never theirs.

## How we want to work on it

Start from one real request type we all recognise, not the whole universe of requests. Prove the idea
on that one before imagining the rest. Keep it something a requester would actually tolerate using.

## Constraints we already know

It must not add more steps than the friction it removes. It should work from plain language, since
requesters will not learn a form. We can use realistic fake requests today; we do not need live data.
