# Team context — Seamless PPA journey for a hybrid solar asset (worked example)

Derived from Donna's framing and the current-state captures. Vet and correct.

## The ambition (one sentence)

A client can model a PPA for a hybrid solar asset as one coherent journey, instead of re-entering the
same asset across Solaris, Chronos and Lumus with the connections between them lossy or broken.

## Who are the users

- **External clients** valuing a PPA for a hybrid solar-plus-storage asset (developers, investors,
  advisory clients).
- **Client Success (CS)**, who today bridge the gap manually — they get the client's generation
  profile into Lumus behind the scenes, because clients cannot do it themselves.

## What does success look like

The client experiences one product, defines the asset once, and their *real* generation profile and
asset context reach the contract valuation — without CS intervention and without falling back to a
generic profile. Concretely for this prototype: the mock makes the journey legible enough to start a
real team conversation about closing the breaks.

## How we want to work on it

Front-stage journey first; backend deferred. A throwaway mock to provoke the conversation, not a build.

## Constraints we already know

Representative data only; no backend integration; nothing client-confidential. Real product names are
fine internally but a consideration before any public sharing.
