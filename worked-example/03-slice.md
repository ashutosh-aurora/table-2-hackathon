# Slice — Three-screen seamless-journey mock (worked example)

## The slice

A clickable, throwaway walkthrough of the three steps as one flow, for a single Iberian
solar-plus-storage asset, that visibly shows:

1. the solar asset and its generation profile (with metadata) carried **intact** into Chronos, against
   today's name-only, metadata-lost hand-off;
2. the same profile present in Lumus pay-as-produced with **no CS step and no generic fallback**;
3. **consistent date entry** with an inline validation example: a contract start cannot precede the
   project CoD.

Static, representative content. No backend.

## Why this slice

It tells the whole vision in three screens and contrasts directly with the three real breaks, which is
what makes it a conversation-starter. It needs no data integration, so it fits a throwaway build.

## What it proves

- **Risk:** usability (and value as a conversation-starter).
- **Question:** does seeing the coherent journey make the teams agree the breaks are worth closing?

## Acceptance check

Someone who has lived the current three-product flow watches the walkthrough and recognises both the
coherence and the specific breaks it removes, and it provokes a concrete next conversation.

## Out of scope

Backend integration, real data, model correctness, any product other than the three in the journey.
