## Intended interface

- **Stage:** Frame (stage 3 of the offsite arc).
- **Inputs:** the agreed ambition; the business, product-area and team context in this folder; the
  glossary.
- **Behaviour:** turn the ambition into a concise problem statement, keeping it in problem and outcome
  terms rather than implementation terms. Use available context to avoid reinventing or conflicting.
  Ask clarifying questions where the problem is thin or ambiguous.
- **Output:** a filled `frame.md` (template in `templates/frame.md`): problem, intended outcome,
  boundaries (in and out of scope), success criteria, and the product risks that are scariest.

## Stand-in behaviour until replaced

If the real skill is not yet available, walk the team through `templates/frame.md` field by field,
asking a clarifying question for each, and grounding answers in the context already in the folder. If
the context needed for an answer is absent or unclear, name the specific gap and who the team should
ask, and record it; do not assume an answer to keep moving.
