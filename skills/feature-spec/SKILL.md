# Intended interface

- **Inputs:** the agreed ambition; the business, product-area and team context in this repository
- **Behaviour:** turn the ambition into a concise problem statement, keeping it in problem and outcome
  terms rather than implementation terms. Use available context to avoid reinventing or conflicting.
  Ask clarifying questions where the problem is thin or ambiguous.
- **Output:** a filled `frame.md` (template in `templates/frame.md`) written to the `feature` folder: problem, intended outcome,
  boundaries (in and out of scope), success criteria, and the product risks that are scariest.


# What to do

Walk the team through `templates/frame.md` field by field

Interview team relentlessly about every aspect of this spec until we reach a shared understanding. For each question, provide your recommended answer and make use of the context in this repository.

Ensure you use the definitions in the glossary to keep the language consistent.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing.

If a question can be answered by exploring context in this repo, explore the context instead.

Once completed, handover to the `shape-to-slice` skill to pick a slice of the problem to prototype.
