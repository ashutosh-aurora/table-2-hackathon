## What Aurora wants to realise

Aurora wants to become a platform company for energy-transition decision-making: integrated,
AI-accelerated, and productised, without surrendering the regional rigour that makes the numbers
trustworthy, or the advisory relationships that substantiate that trust through human ties.

## The tension to hold

The ambition carries a deliberate tension, and it matters when shaping work:

- **Scale:** integrated, AI-accelerated, productised. A platform.
- **Trust, not surrendered:** regional rigour (what makes the numbers trustworthy) and advisory
  relationships (the human ties that substantiate the trust).

Work that buys scale at the cost of regional rigour or advisory trust is off-strategy.
