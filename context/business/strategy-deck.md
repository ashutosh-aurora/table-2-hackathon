### Driving efficiencies through AI across the Product Development Life Cycle

AI is compressing software implementation time. Aurora's response is to build an SDLC that optimises for product shaping, design clarity, and shared context in this new paradigm — rather than simply accelerating the old one.

This means keeping Product, UX (or Modelling) and Engineering in one loop, and adopting a documentation approach that helps both people and AI understand Aurora's products, models, architecture, and regional context. The goal is to realise more value for customers more quickly, by reducing the friction between knowing what to build and building it.
