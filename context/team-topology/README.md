# Team topology and team APIs

**Owner:** each team authors its own team API; together they form the org map. **Audience:** other
teams, and AI agents, that need to coordinate with, route to, or build against a team. This is context
a table **consumes**; it is not authored at the offsite.

- **Team topology** — the map of what teams and functions exist and how they interact.
- **Team API** — one team's progressively-disclosed entry: what it owns, how to engage it, its
  boundaries. Internals stay hidden; only what others need is exposed.

At Aurora the real topology lives in Confluence and in team-interface repositories (for example
`model-and-platform-engineering-information`, the Model-and-Platform Engineering function's team API).

`team-topology.md` here holds a deliberately small, illustrative slice, just enough for the worked
example's triage agent to suggest an owning team. Replace it with the real topology if a table needs
to route to real teams.
